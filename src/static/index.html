How your support assistant works

The assistant is a starting point for policy guidance—not a replacement for university staff or official systems.

01

You ask

Write a question about student support or university policy. Avoid sharing passwords or sensitive personal details.

02

Safety comes first

The API checks for requests outside its support role before answering.

03

Policy sources are searched

The agent looks for relevant passages in its available policy collection. References appear below the answer.

04

You get guidance—or a referral

If sources don’t support an answer, the assistant says so and may recommend contacting the appropriate university office.

What this demo does not do

It does not authenticate students or connect to official student records. Status and ticket workflows use demo data and are not official submissions.

# Project Charter

## Purpose

Define a trustworthy student support assistant that helps students find and understand approved Makerere University information.

## Initial outcomes

- Provide concise, source-grounded answers.
- Make uncertainty and next steps visible.
- Escalate sensitive or institutionally binding cases to the right human channel.

## Out of scope

The agent does not make admissions, academic, disciplinary, financial, health, or legal decisions.

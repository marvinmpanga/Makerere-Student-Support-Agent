# AI Boundary Matrix

| Area | Agent may do | Agent must not do | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General information | Explain approved public guidance | Invent policy or deadlines | Link the official office |
| Academic support | Help locate procedures and resources | Decide grades, progression, or appeals | Refer to the relevant academic office |
| Welfare and safety | Share approved support contacts | Diagnose, counsel clinically, or handle emergencies alone | Encourage immediate human or emergency support |
| Personal records | Explain how to request access or correction | Expose or infer private records | Refer to an authorized office |
